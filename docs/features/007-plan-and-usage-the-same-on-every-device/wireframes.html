Pro and its switch

Where the plan lives in Settings, and every plan page the owner can meet, each with its switch off and on, for the App Store release: the same on the Mac, the iPhone and the iPad. The words and bars are a proposal, built on the owner's distinction: Pro is what you have; the switch is whether you use it right now.

Every deviceMac, iPhone and iPad buy alikeFrom the App Store, on each. Bought on one, restored on the others — never borrowed through iCloud. The same card everywhere.
What you haveFree, Plus, Pro — and top-upsBought in the App Store. Pro and top-ups both give the bigger AI. Shown as bars whenever you have any.
Whether you use itThe switch: “Use Pro”Off keeps everything you have. Apple's model on this device reads what comes in, and nothing leaves it. The bars stay, greyed.
The top-up buttonWith Plus or Pro, alwaysGrey while there is plenty; filled black once the month and top-ups together are a fifth of a Pro month or less. With Free, today: “See the plans” instead — proposed in C: the top-up's own button too.
The rulePro or top-ups: always a barEven before the server has counted anything: then an empty bar that says so.

While buying, and after
Buying: the button waits, the App Store's own sheet asks. Waits for approval: “The purchase waits for approval — Ask to Buy, or the bank.” Cancelled: nothing changes, nothing is said. Could not be confirmed: “The App Store could not confirm the purchase. Nothing was charged that is not confirmed.” Bought: the sheet closes; the card is D or E. A top-up bought turns Use Pro on by itself, today. On another device: the same plan after Restore, or by itself at the next start.

For you to decide

  1. The switch's name. “Use Pro” keeps Pro as what you have and the switch as using it. Today it is “Pro on”.
  2. Off keeps the bars, greyed, “not in use”. Or hide them while off?
  3. Top-ups without Pro (C). The corner says why: “Free”, “Plus · renews …”, or “Pro ended 7 November”. Or the card's name becomes “Causabee · top-ups”?
  4. Not counted yet (G). An empty bar with “not counted yet”, as you said: Pro is never without a bar.
  5. A top-up bought turns Use Pro on by itself. Keep that, now that the switch is your decision?
  6. Manage subscription on the Mac too, once it buys — the App Store's own sheet there.
  7. Where it lives. One row “Plan and usage” at the top of Settings, opening its page; on the Mac a “Plan” pane. “Use Pro” also as a row under it — or only on the page and the bee?
  8. What F007 holds. The row and the page, the switch's name and its greyed state, the cases above. Buying on the Mac needs the Mac in the App Store — its own piece of work, before or after?
