## On each device

- **The film:** one film for every device. Safari on the Mac, iPad and iPhone plays it in the website's player, as today: 1080p, or 720p where the page picks it.
- **Inside the film:** the Mac, the iPad and the iPhone each show today's app on that device, from the same demo. One matter, one state (principle 12).

## Undo, and what the user can take back

Not needed: a film has nothing to take back. Until the owner says "publish", the film online stays as it is.

## Accessibility

- **Captions:** in the player (`walkthrough.vtt`) and as `.srt`, word for word the voice. They are for anyone who plays it without sound.
- **The poster:** the frame at 5 s, as today. Its alt text names what it shows.
- **The video's label on causabee.app:** unchanged. If a scene it names is changed, its words are corrected with F003, not here.
