A new beta of Causabee for Mac.

Causabee turns what comes in — mail, screenshots, photos, files and scans, notes, what you say in the chat — into clear matters: what comes next, who does what, and by when.

## New in this beta

- **Dates you set stay set.** A time or how long a date lasts, set in the chat or by hand, stays. Calendar and what comes in later no longer put it back. A date shows from when to when and how long: "14:30–17:30 · 3 h". You set how long it lasts, not when it ends.
- **The chat changes exactly what you name.** Before, a card could change another item: "Change date" on an appointment changed an unrelated task, and "Set again" closed the app. If something changed it back since, the card says so and offers "Set again".
- **Full control from the chat.** Put away, open again, change one detail, move to another matter, close a matter, keep a decision. Each can be undone. A card that cannot do something says why.
- **Messages in your voice.** Drafts follow the way the other side writes and use the language of the exchange. They greet a person by first name, or by title and surname. A change you ask for in a draft is made, and keeps its recipient.
- **One AI for everything:** GPT-6.1 Sol, with Causabee Pro.
- **Waiting is not doing.** What others still have to do stands apart from your own tasks.
- **The film on causabee.app shows Causabee as it is today.**

## Try it without setting anything up

At the end of the introduction, click **Try the demo**. Causabee opens made-up matters — a trip, a move, care for a parent and more — kept apart from your own. **Leave the demo** in the sidebar takes you back. You can also find both in the Causabee menu.

## Install

1. Download **{{FILE}}** below and open it.
2. In the window that opens, drag **Causabee** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open Causabee from your Applications folder.

The app and the disk image are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## What you need

- macOS Sequoia 15 or later
- For your mail: a Gmail, iCloud or other IMAP account, and a label on the mail Causabee should read (for example “Causabee”). Gmail needs an app password. Screenshots, photos, files and notes need nothing more.
- **Causabee Pro** for the summary, the chat, and an AI that reads what comes in. Without Pro, Apple's model on your Mac sorts what comes in, and nothing leaves it.

## Privacy

What comes in stays on your devices; a matter's files stay on your devices or in a folder of your own iCloud Drive. Before anything goes to the AI, names, addresses and numbers are replaced. With Pro, the disguised text goes through Causabee's server to OpenAI and the answer comes back. Causabee has no analytics. [Privacy policy](https://causabee.app/privacy.html)

## iCloud

In **Settings › iCloud**, choose **On** and start Causabee again: your matters — tasks, dates, people, a short summary of what came in and the chat's history — sync through your own private iCloud to your iPhone, iPad and other Macs. The full texts of mail and scans go too, end-to-end encrypted; files stay in each matter’s folder. **Off** keeps everything on this Mac.
