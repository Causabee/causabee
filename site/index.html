A trip. A move. Care for a parent. Each one arrives in pieces: a mail here, a PDF there, a screenshot you meant to keep. Matterbee puts the pieces together.

Your notes

What only you know, kept right beside the facts.

Nothing slips through.

Tasks, dates, files and people, pulled from your mail and put where they belong.

Who does what, and by when.

Tasks are yours, shared, or someone else’s you’re waiting for. Some can only start after another.

Calendar and Reminders, in step.

Files, links and people.

Attachments, shared documents and everyone involved, each with their role.

Every fact has a source.

Each task and date links back to the mail it came from. Check anything in one click.

Get new mail

Label a mail “Matterbee” in Gmail or iCloud. It’s sorted in on the next fetch.

Drop anything in

Drag in a mail, a PDF or a screenshot. Matterbee finds the matter it belongs to.

A folder for each matter

Its files, with names you can read, in iCloud Drive or a folder you choose.

In your language
