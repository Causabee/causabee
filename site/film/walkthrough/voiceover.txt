This is Causabee. Mail, letters, documents, screenshots — it turns all the pieces into clear matters.

Let's follow one of them — Mum's care, after her fall — through one day, on the Mac, the iPad, and the iPhone.

In the morning, you open your Mac. With Auto on, Causabee has already fetched the new mail, and read it.

Before anything went to the AI, names and numbers were swapped for placeholders, right on your Mac. Dr. Brandt became Person A, and Helga became Person B. The AI only ever sees the disguise.

All that's left for you is the last step: take it in.

The two mails land in Mum's matter, and they bring a new task with them.

Every matter has one next step, and the reason it can't wait.

Below it are the tasks: whose each one is, when it's due, and which mail it came from.

In the afternoon, you're at Mum's, with the iPad. Tick off what's done.

Through your own iCloud, it's ticked off on your iPhone and your Mac as well.

There's paper, too. A letter from Mum's health insurer: her own share of the hospital stay.

Scan it with your iPhone. Causabee reads it, files the letter in Mum's matter, and turns it into a task: pay her share by the end of the month.

On the way home, pull down on your iPhone. The bee flies in, fetches your mail, and sorts each one into its matter.

Then just ask: what do I need to do before Tuesday's visit?

The answer comes with its sources. And a good suggestion becomes a task, with one tap.

In the evening, back at the Mac, it's all there: what you ticked off on the iPad, and what you added on the iPhone.

Your mail stays in your mail account. Your documents and your matters go with you, through your own iCloud.

Causabee. Be organized. Be private.
